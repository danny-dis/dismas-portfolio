## What This Tells Us

1. **The market doesn't care about AGI hype.** Developers use what works, what's fast, and what's cheap.

2. **Chinese labs are winning on availability and pricing.** When Hy4 Preview offers comparable quality to GPT-5.6 Luna at better rates, developers switch.

3. **Free models are viable for most tasks.** Nemotron 3 and MiniMax M3 aren't as good as Astra or Fable 5.1, but they're good enough for 80% of use cases.

4. **The "best" model is the one that's actually available.** Rate limits, access restrictions, and regional availability matter more than benchmark scores.

5. **OpenAI and Anthropic are overpriced for what most developers need.** GPT-5.6 Luna at $0.20/M is the sweet spot. Astra at $10/M needs to be 50x better to justify the cost — and it's not.

## The Real Take

The OpenRouter rankings reveal a truth the labs don't want to admit: **most developers don't need frontier models.** They need fast, cheap, reliable models that handle the task at hand.

The labs are racing toward AGI. The market is racing toward efficiency. Those aren't the same thing.

And right now, the market is winning.
