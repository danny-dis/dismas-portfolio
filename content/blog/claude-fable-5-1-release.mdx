# Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1: Same Brain, Different Leash

**Anthropic released Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 on September 1, 2026 — four days before GPT-6 Astra. The strategy is clear: one model, two products.**

## The Architecture

Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 are **identical under the hood** — same weights, same capabilities. The difference is entirely in the safeguard layer:

- **Claude Fable 5.1** — general availability, additional safeguards for cybersecurity and biology
- **Claude Mythos 5.1** — restricted access, more permissive safeguards for vetted organizations

Mythos 5.1 is available through:
- **Project Glasswing** (Anthropic's trusted access program)
- **Cyber Verification Program** (currently US-only)
- **Life Sciences Verification Program** (in partnership with the US government)

## Pricing: The Real News

Per-token pricing stayed the same: **$10 input / $50 output per million tokens**. But cache reads dropped **75% to $0.25 per million tokens**.

This is huge for long-running agentic sessions. A coding agent that re-reads a 200,000-token cached prefix 50 times costs:
- Fable 5: $10.00
- Opus 5: $5.00
- **Fable 5.1: $2.50**

Anthropic reports practical savings of ~25% for typical workloads, up to ~45% for highly agentic ones.

But there's a catch: Artificial Analysis found Fable 5.1 uses **1.7x more output tokens** than Fable 5 at max effort, making it **20% more expensive per task** despite the cache discount.

## What's New in Fable 5.1

### Vulnerability Discovery Now Allowed

Fable 5.1 can now **discover software vulnerabilities** in source code — something Fable 5 blocked entirely. It still blocks:
- Exploit generation
- Penetration testing
- Binary-based vulnerability scanning

The cybersecurity safeguards now block **60% fewer false positives** than Fable 5 at launch. Users on Claude Code can expect ~60% fewer interventions per session.

### Mythos 5.1: The Unleashed Version

With cybersecurity safeguards off, Mythos 5.1 demonstrates **the strongest cyber capabilities of any model Anthropic has released** — meeting or exceeding Mythos 5 across:
- ExploitBench
- OSS-Fuzz
- Firefox 147
- ExploitGym

Anthropic says Mythos 5.1 still **falls short of CB-2** (their next risk tier), meaning it can't fully substitute for top human talent. But critics disagree — Zvi Substack noted it's "likely to be Tier 2, similar to Astra."

## User Feedback

Early reviews are mixed:

**The good:**
- "A big improvement in style, sounds less stereotypically like other Claude models" — Felix Rieseberg, Anthropic employee
- Follows style instructions more reliably
- Huge leap on science benchmarks (doubled Terminal-Bench-Science score)

**The bad:**
- Some users report burning through multi-hour session quotas in ~20 minutes
- Hallucination regression — attempts answers more often when it's wrong
- Rate limits and subscription UX frustrations
- Data retention (30-day default) keeps it out of regulated industries

**The expensive:**
- At $10/$50, it's double Opus 5's pricing
- The cache discount helps, but increased output token usage offsets savings for many workloads

## The Bottom Line

Fable 5.1 is Anthropic's best coding model yet, and the cache pricing makes it genuinely attractive for long-running agentic work. But it's not the slam-dunk win the benchmarks suggest — real-world costs are higher than expected, and the capability gains over Fable 5 are concentrated in specific domains (science, long-horizon coding) rather than across the board.

Mythos 5.1 is where the real power lives, but access is gated by geography and vetting. If you're a non-US defender, you're out of luck for now.
