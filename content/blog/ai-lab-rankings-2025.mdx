# The AI Lab Rankings September 2025: Every Model From Every Lab, Ranked

**September 2025 was the biggest month in AI history. Two frontier flagships, a dozen near-frontier challengers, and the gap between "best" and "best value" has never been wider.**

Here's every significant model from every major lab, ranked by capability, pricing, and real-world usage.

---

## Tier 1: The Frontier (The Best Period)

These are the most capable models ever built. Full stop.

**The reasoning king.**

- Abstract reasoning and novel problem-solving (ARC-AGI-3)

**The verdict:** If you're solving problems in unfamiliar domains, Astra is worth the premium. For everything else, it's overpriced.

### 2. Claude Fable 5.1 + Mythos 5.1 (Anthropic) — September 1, 2026

**The coding and knowledge work champion.**

Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 are the **same model with different safeguards**. Mythos removes cybersecurity and biology restrictions for vetted organizations.

| Benchmark | Fable 5.1 | Mythos 5.1 |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 | 52.6% | — |
| Terminal-Bench 4.0 | 55.8% | 60.9% |
| OSWorld 2.0 (partial) | 77.9% | — |
| Humanity's Last Exam (no tools) | 60.9% | — |
| AutomationBench | 31.4% | — |
| Coding Agent Index | 70 | — |
| Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index | 66 | — |

**Pricing:** $10/$50 per million tokens
**Cache reads:** $0.25 per million (75% discount)

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class coding agent (Coding Agent Index: 70)
- 75% cache discount for long sessions
- Improved prose style over Fable 5

**The verdict:** For long-running coding sessions with heavy context reuse, Fable 5.1's cache pricing wins. For raw capability, Astra edges it out.

## The Verdict: Who's Actually Winning?

**On raw capability:** OpenAI (Astra) and Anthropic (Fable 5.1/Mythos 5.1) are neck and neck. Astra wins on reasoning; Fable wins on coding agents.

**On value:** DeepSeek is obliterating the competition. V4 Flash at $0.28/M delivers 80% of frontier capability.

**On availability:** Chinese labs (Alibaba, Tencent, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Zhipu) are shipping faster, with fewer restrictions, at lower prices.

**On real-world usage:** The market doesn't care about AGI hype. Developers use what's cheap, fast, and available.

**The uncomfortable truth:** For 90% of use cases, GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 are overkill. DeepSeek V4 Flash or Qwen3.7-Plus handle the workload at a fraction of the cost.

But for that last 10% — the truly novel, truly hard, truly unprecedented problems — Astra and Fable 5.1 are the only tools that work. And that's worth paying for.
